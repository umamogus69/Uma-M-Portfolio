Projects

A few things I've worked on recently.

Stock Market Simulation Interactive Qualifying Project

Built an interactive stock-market simulation and experimentation framework to evaluate forecasting approaches for volatile equities. Compared symbolic regression, time-series forecasting, and neural-network models across historical price data, measuring predictive accuracy, robustness, and interpretability.

Developed a reproducible workflow for data preparation, model training, simulation, and performance analysis, then investigated model behavior during volatile market conditions. Findings were synthesized into a formal report on the promise and limitations of AI-driven stock forecasting, including implications for risk communication and responsible decision support.

ToMVerse of Madness: A Theory of Mind Simulation in LLMs

Evaluated large language models and LLM agents on Theory of Mind tasks. Developed standardized benchmarking prompts and evaluation pipelines to measure social reasoning capabilities, and authored the findings in a research paper accepted for publication at the IEEE MIT Undergraduate Research Technology Conference (URTC).
